**Theme colour.** Your tokens are `--color-background`, `--color-ink`, `--color-accent`. Tailwind v4 turns those into CSS variables, so `/data` can override them under a wrapper like `[data-theme="data"]`. Any component that uses `bg-background` or `text-accent` then changes automatically. The catch is that anything with a hard-coded blue (a hex value or `bg-blue-*`) won't follow, and I expect the backdrops to be the main offenders. Keeping it light with one accent, like the rest of your site, I'd suggest:

| Option | Background | Accent | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teal-green | `#e8f6f1` | `#064e3b` | Analytical, calm, clearly different from blue |
| Warm amber | `#fdf3e1` | `#7c2d12` | Warm and distinctive, reads as "dashboard" |
| Violet | `#f0ecfb` | `#2e1065` | Closest to your blue, so safest if you want it to feel related |

I'd pick teal-green: it's the strongest contrast with the blue and fits data work. I can tune the exact shades once I see the site.
